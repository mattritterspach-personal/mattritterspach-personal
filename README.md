## Methodology

**My AI consulting framework has three phases:**

**1. Assess** — Map current state: data maturity, process complexity, team capability, and risk tolerance. Most companies are further along than they think in some areas and significantly behind in others.

**2. Prioritize** — Identify the 3-5 AI use cases with the highest ROI-to-effort ratio. Avoid the trap of chasing the most impressive use case instead of the most valuable one.

**3. Execute** — Build proof-of-concept, measure against baseline, then scale what works. Every engagement produces working artifacts — not slide decks.
